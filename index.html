About the tool

Terrace Clips is a personal utility that takes short clips edited by the operator of the TikTok account @sunrise.terrace and sends them to that account's drafts through TikTok's Content Posting API. It does one job and serves one account.

Eligibility

The tool is used solely by the operator of @sunrise.terrace. It is not a product, has no sign up, and no one else can connect it to an account. Seeing this page from a consent screen means you are the operator approving your own tool.

Drafts, never posts

Uploads land as drafts. The tool has no ability to publish, schedule a publish, or change anything already on the account. The decision to post, and the caption and sound on every post, stays with you in the app.

Rules the operator follows

Only clips the operator has the rights to use are uploaded. The tool does not touch other accounts, does not automate comments, follows or likes, and gathers no information about any person.

Warranty and risk

Provided as is, with no promise it keeps working. If TikTok's API changes, the tool may stop. That affects the operator alone.

Revisions

When the tool changes, this page changes with it. The effective date marks the current version.
